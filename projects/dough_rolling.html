Force-Aware Robotic Dough Manipulation

Exploring force feedback and iterative control for uniform dough rolling using a robotic manipulator.

Harshada Kale·IIT Gandhinagar

Overview

This research project explores robotic dough rolling using a Franka robotic arm, a force/torque sensor, and an Intel RealSense camera. The objective is to investigate how force feedback and visual measurements can help adapt rolling behavior when working with deformable materials.

The system combines dough localization, Cartesian trajectory tracking, and force control. After each rolling pass, the measured profile is used to generate an updated force profile for the next pass, creating an iterative approach to improving thickness uniformity.

Current work focuses on evaluating force-tracking behavior and improving thickness estimation through vision. The longer-term goal is to establish a reliable feedback-driven framework for robotic manipulation of deformable materials.

Research Focus

  • Force-feedback control for deformable materials
  • Iterative force-profile adaptation
  • Vision-based dough thickness estimation
