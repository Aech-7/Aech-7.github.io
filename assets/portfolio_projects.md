# Projects

## 1. 4. Team RUDRA --- DD-Robocon

Designed robotic mechanisms for game-object handling, robot lifting,
weapon assembly, and autonomous navigation as part of Team RUDRA for
National DD-Robocon.

## 2. 5. VR-Based Hand Rehabilitation Glove

Developed a wearable glove using flex sensors to track finger motion in
real time and translate it into gamified VR exercises for hand
rehabilitation.

## 3. 6. Wire-Tracing Robot

Taught a robot to trace a 3D wire path through gravity-compensated
demonstration, trajectory processing, DLS inverse kinematics, and
velocity-controlled replay.

## 4. 7. Peg Sorting & Mobile Peg Insertion

Built robotic manipulation pipelines for identifying and sorting pegs
using color and shape perception, kinematics, path planning, and
trajectory generation, and extended the task to precise peg insertion
using mobile manipulation and workspace analysis.

## 5. 8. Robot Pick-and-Place --- Simulation to Hardware

Developed a pick-and-place workflow that progressed from randomized
MuJoCo simulation and inverse-kinematics comparison to physical robot
execution and Cartesian control.

## 6. 10. Towfish Cable Dynamics

Developed a lumped-mass model of a towed underwater cable and compared
numerical approaches for predicting its steady-state and dynamic
configuration under hydrodynamic forces.

## 7. 11. Multimodal Sentiment Analysis

Built a cost-efficient labeling pipeline combining human annotation,
weak supervision, active learning, and LLM-based labeling for text and
speech sentiment data.

## 8. 12. SVD-Based Image Watermarking

Implemented SVD-based QR watermark embedding and recovery, then
evaluated the trade-off between image quality and robustness under noise
and JPEG compression.

## 9. 13. Classical Computer Vision from Scratch

Implemented image-processing and feature-detection methods from scratch,
then built template- and feature-based object detectors robust to scale,
rotation, noise, and occlusion.

## 10. 14. Human Activity Recognition with Decision Trees

Implemented a Decision Tree classifier from scratch and used
accelerometer data, feature engineering, PCA, and nested
cross-validation to study activity recognition and the trade-off between
model complexity and performance.
